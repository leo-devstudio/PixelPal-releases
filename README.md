## 💡 What is PixelPal?

PixelPal transforms your phone, tablet, or secondary device into a powerful, customizable macro controller for your PC. 

The **PixelPal Desktop Companion** runs quietly in your Windows system tray, bridging your mobile controls to your PC actions instantly over your local network.

---

## ✨ Features

- ⚡ **Ultra-low latency** connection over your local Wi-Fi.
- 🎛️ **Custom Action Grid**: Trigger hotkeys, media controls, and shortcuts.
- 🎥 **OBS Studio & Streaming Controls**: Switch scenes, toggle sources, and manage audio.
- 🔔 **System Tray Integration**: Starts with Windows and runs quietly in the background without cluttering your taskbar.
- 🔒 **Local & Secure**: All commands run locally on your Wi-Fi network—no cloud accounts required.

## 📱 How to Connect Your Device

1. Make sure your PC and mobile device are on the **same Wi-Fi network**.
2. Right-click the **PixelPal** icon in your Windows system tray.
3. Open the web dashboard or scan the connection QR code from your mobile device.
4. Start controlling your PC!
